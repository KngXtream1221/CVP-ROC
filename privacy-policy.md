# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 12 August 2026

This policy explains what data the bot ("the Bot") handles and why. It applies to the Bot only, not to Discord itself or to any Roblox experience the Bot connects to.

## Who runs this

The Bot is operated by an individual, not a company. Contact details are at the bottom of this document.

## What data the Bot handles

The Bot receives and acts on the following:

- **Discord user IDs** — the numeric ID of a member being moved between voice channels.
- **Discord channel and server IDs** — to identify where a member should be moved to.
- **Roblox user IDs and usernames** — where an action originates from a linked Roblox experience.

The Bot does **not** read, store, or process message content, voice audio, email addresses, IP addresses, or payment information.

## How that data is used

Data is used for one purpose: moving a Discord member from one voice channel to another in response to an in-game event, and recording that an action took place.

It is not used for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making, and it is not sold or shared with third parties.

## Retention

Requests are processed in memory and are not written to a persistent database. Operational logs may briefly retain user IDs and request outcomes for debugging; these are not retained long-term and are not used for any other purpose.

Where an action is logged to a Discord channel via webhook, that message persists in the channel until deleted by a server administrator. Those logs are under the control of the server's administrators, not the Bot operator.

If account linking is enabled, the mapping between a Discord user ID and a Roblox user ID is stored for as long as the link is active, and deleted when the user unlinks or on request.

## Third parties

The Bot runs on Cloudflare Workers and communicates with the Discord API. Data passing through those services is subject to their own privacy policies.

Where account linking uses a third-party verification service, that service's privacy policy applies to the lookup it performs.

## Your choices

You can ask for any stored link between your Discord and Roblox accounts to be deleted at any time by contacting the operator. Leaving the Discord server, or removing the Bot from it, ends all processing relating to you.

## Children

The Bot is not directed at children under 13, and Discord's own Terms of Service require users to be at least 13 years old.

This policy may be updated. The date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
